Working together

Clear thinking.
Open communication.
High standards.

I work directly, openly and with a strong focus on solutions. I ask questions, bring structure to complexity and challenge ideas when there is a better answer.

I am equally comfortable discussing the bigger picture and getting into the details when needed. No theatre. No design for design’s sake — just good thinking, strong work and a shared ambition to make things better.
