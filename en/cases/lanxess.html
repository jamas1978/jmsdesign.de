Work 05 / LANXESS

LANXESS

Making the
unseen visible.

An awareness campaign revealing where LANXESS materials play a role in mobility — connecting technical expertise with recognisable products and applications.

Role
Creative Concept & Art Direction
Agency
Liquid Campaign
Client
LANXESS

Challenge

Technical materials and industrial expertise often remain invisible to the people who benefit from them. For LANXESS, the communication challenge was to make the company’s role in mobility tangible by showing where its materials appear in recognisable products and applications.

My role

Working for Liquid Campaign, I helped develop the visual campaign concept for LANXESS Green Mobility, including the green-city key visual. I conceived and designed campaign motifs and special formats, supported their execution and helped safeguard visual quality.

Approach

In collaboration with Liquid Campaign, I translated the mobility theme into an accessible visual language, from the green-city key visual to application-led campaign motifs and special formats. The work extended into large-format outdoor communication, including Davos.

Outcome

The resulting visual campaign work made an abstract B2B topic accessible across different motifs and formats, with a consistent design approach from concept through execution.
