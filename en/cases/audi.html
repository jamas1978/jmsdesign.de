Challenge

Audi’s corporate communications bring together a wide range of topics — business performance, transformation, technology, products and executive communication. The challenge was to make complex content clear and visually compelling across different channels and formats, while staying true to Audi’s established brand guidelines.

My role

Following the successful pitch, I worked as a freelance Creative Director on Audi projects within the Palmer Hargreaves team for around three years. I was responsible for the creative and visual direction of the communications projects handled by the agency. I developed concepts, set the design direction and guided an Art Director, a designer and a production artist through execution. Working closely with copywriting, editorial and account teams, I presented concepts and design solutions directly to Audi and its specialist departments, representing the creative direction in client meetings.

Approach

Before the first design, I worked with the client to clarify the actual communication challenge: What needs to come across, to whom and in which format? From there, I developed creative approaches, aligned them with copywriting, editorial and account teams, and gave the creative team a shared visual direction for execution. This made room for different perspectives while keeping clarity, quality and Audi’s brand identity at the centre — from management presentations and the Annual Media Conference to financial communication and social media.

Outcome

The team delivered executive presentations, financial infographics, press materials and corporate social formats. For the Annual Media Conference, elements from my original visual concept became part of the final design.

Visual
direction.

For the Annual Media Conference, I developed three creative directions. My preferred concept drew on the geometry of Audi’s lighting design, creating a visual system for presentations, infographics, press and social communication. Initially selected, the direction was later combined with elements of another proposal. These visuals show my original approach; only elements were carried into the final execution.
