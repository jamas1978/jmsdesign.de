METRO / HORECA SELECT

Communication across
professional gastronomy.

Campaign ideas, product communication and promotional formats for professional food-service customers — from the Professional Kitchen Line and The Chefs’ Choice to target-group-specific activations for pizza and delivery businesses.

Role
Creative Concept & Art Direction
Agency
Liquid Campaign
Client
METRO / HORECA Select

Challenge

HORECA Select addressed professional customers with very different needs across gastronomy. The communication therefore had to work beyond a single product category or promotion — from professional kitchen equipment to specific business situations and target groups.

My role

For Liquid Campaign, I took responsibility for concept and design across METRO / HORECA Select communication. The brief was to find new ways of promoting products and assortments — through mailings, in-store campaigns, flyers and unconventional promotional formats.

Approach

Professional gastronomy is not one uniform audience. A hotel kitchen has different needs from a pizza or delivery business, and the communication should reflect that. This perspective informed the work across the Professional Kitchen Line, The Chefs’ Choice and targeted promotions — including the pizza box as a communication medium. Rather than applying one generic retail message, each format addressed a specific working context while keeping the products and their value clear.

Outcome

The result was a broader body of B2B communication that connected HORECA Select products and services with different professional use cases instead of relying on one generic retail message.
