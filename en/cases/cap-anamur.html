Challenge

Donation communication needs to create emotional relevance while remaining credible, clear and recognisable. For Cap Anamur, the challenge was to translate different dimensions of humanitarian support into one simple and repeatable campaign principle.

My role

Working directly with Cap Anamur, I developed the campaign concept and creative direction without an agency intermediary. The work involved translating the organisation’s communication goals into a clear verbal and visual system and developing the campaign together with the client from concept through execution.

Approach

The key creative decision was to put what humanitarian help makes possible at the centre, rather than describing individual measures. This became a simple verbal principle: Hoffnung, Freude, Chancen, Zukunft and Leben, each paired with the call to “spenden”. Combined with documentary photography and bold typography, the idea formed a flexible campaign system that could carry different messages without losing its recognisable character.

Outcome

One verbal and visual system supported five donation messages: hope, joy, opportunity, a future and life. The shared typography and documentary photography gave the series a consistent identity.
