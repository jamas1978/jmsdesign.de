MINI Clubman

The Other
MINI.

Campaign development and international adaptation for the MINI Clubman — from creative concepts to market-specific executions across the Caribbean, Africa and the Gulf region, including Dubai and the UAE.

Role
Creative Concept & Art Direction
Agency
Interone BBDO
Client
MINI

Challenge

Developing a MINI Clubman campaign was only part of the task. The creative idea also had to work across different international markets, including the Caribbean, Africa and the Gulf region, with their respective communication requirements.

My role

I developed creative routes and visual concepts independently, taking responsibility for both concept and design. I also guided a junior art director in the creative work. Client communication was handled by the agency.

Approach

I developed creative routes and campaign motifs, then adapted the work for different markets and formats — including the Caribbean, Africa, Dubai and other markets in the United Arab Emirates. The challenge was to respond to local requirements while retaining the distinctive MINI tone and visual identity.

Outcome

The MINI Clubman campaign was adapted for markets in the Caribbean, Africa and the United Arab Emirates, including Dubai. The local executions retained the shared creative direction and MINI tone.
