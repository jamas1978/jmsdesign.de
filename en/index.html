How I work

The more complex the challenge, the clearer the idea.

I clarify what the audience needs to understand, develop distinct creative routes and agree on a direction with the people responsible for the brief. Then I guide the team through delivery.

01

Understand

Clarify the objective, the audience and the constraints before developing a solution.

02

Distil

Separate the essential from the incidental, weigh up approaches and define a clear creative route.

03

Make it real

Bring people and disciplines together, provide creative direction and ensure quality and consistency from concept to execution.

Profile

I have over 25 years of experience in brand and corporate communications, working in agency teams and directly with clients. I develop campaign concepts and visual systems for complex subjects and keep the work consistent across formats.

I set the creative direction, discuss the options with clients and bring design, copy and editorial teams together. I stay close to the work, give clear feedback and make decisions when the team needs them.
