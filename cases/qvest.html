QVEST

Brand rollout,
made usable.

Turning strategic brand principles into scalable applications — across social media, presentations, publications and the everyday tools teams actually need.

Role
Freelance Creative Consultant
Agency
JMS Design
Client
QVEST

Challenge

The introduction of a new corporate identity only works when it becomes usable in everyday communication. For QVEST, the challenge was to translate strategic brand principles into practical applications across different teams, formats and communication needs without losing consistency.

My role

As Freelance Creative Consultant, I supported the implementation of the new brand across a broad range of applications. My focus was on turning the identity from a design concept into a working system that could be used consistently in day-to-day communication.

Approach

The brand principles were translated into scalable applications for social media, presentations, publications and other corporate assets. The work balanced visual consistency with practical usability, ensuring that the brand could function across different formats while remaining recognisable and coherent.

Outcome

The result was a more usable and scalable brand system that enabled the new identity to be applied consistently across internal and external communication.
