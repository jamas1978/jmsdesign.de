Challenge

Audi’s corporate communications bring together a wide range of topics — business performance, transformation, technology, products and executive communication. The challenge was to make complex content clear and visually compelling across different channels and formats, while staying true to Audi’s established brand guidelines.

My role

Following the successful pitch, I worked as part of the Palmer Hargreaves Audi team for around three years, taking creative responsibility for the visual direction of the communications projects handled by the agency. I developed concepts, set the creative direction and guided the execution alongside an Art Director, a designer and a production artist. I collaborated closely with copywriting, editorial and account teams, presenting concepts and design solutions directly to Audi and its specialist departments.

Approach

Before the first design, I worked with the client to clarify the actual communication challenge: What needs to come across, to whom and in which format? From there, I developed creative approaches, aligned them with copywriting, editorial and account teams, and gave the creative team a shared visual direction for execution. This made room for different perspectives while keeping clarity, quality and Audi’s brand identity at the centre — from management presentations and the Annual Media Conference to financial communication and social media.

Outcome

Over three years, our team delivered a broad range of communication formats, making complex subjects clear, consistent and recognisably Audi. The ongoing collaboration allowed me to contribute beyond individual designs, help shape creative decisions and maintain a coherent visual direction across the projects we handled.
