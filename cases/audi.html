Challenge

Audi’s management and corporate communications had to translate complex business, transformation and technology topics into clear and compelling stories for media, stakeholders and digital audiences. The challenge was to make dense content accessible while keeping communication precise, consistent and recognisably Audi.

My role

As Creative Lead, I was responsible for shaping the creative direction and translating strategic and business content into communication formats for management, media and corporate channels. My role connected content, storytelling and visual execution, with a strong focus on clarity and brand coherence.

Approach

The work covered management presentations, financial storytelling, infographics, corporate social formats and product-related communication. Rather than treating each topic as an isolated piece, the focus was on developing visual and narrative principles that could accommodate different content within one coherent system.

Outcome

The result was a flexible communication framework that made complex corporate topics easier to understand and created greater consistency across management, media and social communication.
