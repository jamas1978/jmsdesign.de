A donation campaign built around one direct verbal idea and a consistent visual system across a family of social-impact motifs.

Project
Donation Campaign
Agency
JMS Design

Cap Anamur

Campaign idea

The campaign uses a direct, repeatable verbal principle: human needs and positive outcomes are paired with the call to “spenden” — from Hoffnung and Freude to Chancen, Zukunft and Leben.

Visual system

  • Documentary photography as the emotional core
  • Large diagonal headlines in white and blue
  • Consistent donation information and Cap Anamur signature
  • A system that carries across different subjects without losing recognition

Campaign family

  • Hoffnung spenden
  • Freude spenden
  • Chancen spenden
  • Zukunft spenden
  • Leben spenden

One idea.
Five messages.

The campaign family applies the same typographic and photographic language to different dimensions of humanitarian support.
