Challenge

Donation communication needs to create emotional relevance while remaining credible, clear and recognisable. For Cap Anamur, the challenge was to translate different dimensions of humanitarian support into one simple and repeatable campaign principle.

My role

Working directly with Cap Anamur, I developed the campaign concept and creative direction without an agency intermediary. The work involved translating the organisation’s communication goals into a clear verbal and visual system and developing the campaign together with the client from concept through execution.

Approach

The campaign was built around a simple verbal principle: positive human outcomes such as Hoffnung, Freude, Chancen, Zukunft and Leben were paired with the call to “spenden”. Documentary photography and bold typography created a flexible campaign language that could be applied across different motifs while remaining immediately recognisable.

Outcome

The result was a coherent campaign system that allowed Cap Anamur to communicate different aspects of humanitarian support through one strong and consistent idea.
