Senior Art Director
Agency

Client
METRO / HORECA

Challenge

Professional food-service customers need communication that is relevant to their everyday business rather than generic retail advertising. For METRO / HORECA, the challenge was to continuously develop promotional ideas and new communication formats that connected products, assortments and services with the specific needs of professional target groups.

My role

As Senior Art Director at Liquid Campaign, I worked on the development of campaign ideas, promotional concepts and new communication formats for METRO / HORECA. My role combined concept development, art direction and the translation of commercial offers into communication relevant to professional customers.

Approach

A range of promotional formats and activations was developed around specific assortments, occasions and target groups. One example was a promotion for pizza businesses and delivery services in which the relevant assortment was presented in a brochure placed inside a pizza box — using a familiar object from the audience’s everyday business as the communication medium itself.

Outcome

The work created a series of more targeted and distinctive promotional formats, connecting METRO’s product offer more closely with the realities of professional customers. The pizza-box promotion was one example of this broader approach.
