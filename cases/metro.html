Work 06 / METRO / HORECA SELECT

METRO / HORECA SELECT

Communication across
professional gastronomy.

Campaign ideas, product communication and promotional formats for professional food-service customers — from the Professional Kitchen Line and The Chefs’ Choice to target-group-specific activations for pizza and delivery businesses.

Role
Senior Art Director
Agency
Liquid Campaign
Client
METRO / HORECA Select

Challenge

HORECA Select addressed professional customers with very different needs across gastronomy. The communication therefore had to work beyond a single product category or promotion — from professional kitchen equipment to specific business situations and target groups.

My role

As Senior Art Director at Liquid Campaign, I worked on campaign ideas, product communication and promotional formats for METRO / HORECA Select. My role combined concept development and art direction with the translation of assortments and commercial offers into communication relevant to professional customers.

Approach

The work covered different areas and formats. The examples shown here include the Professional Kitchen Line for hotels, restaurants and caterers, The Chefs’ Choice as a campaign and sales communication line for professional kitchen equipment, and a target-group-specific activation for pizza and delivery businesses. Each format was developed around the context in which professional customers actually work.

Outcome

The result was a broader body of B2B communication that connected HORECA Select products and services with different professional use cases instead of relying on one generic retail message.
