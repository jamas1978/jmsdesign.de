MINI Clubman

The Other
MINI.

International launch communication that kept the MINI idea, image language and tone consistent while adapting the campaign across markets and motifs.

Role
Art Director
Agency
Interone BBDO
Client
MINI

Challenge

An international launch campaign has to remain unmistakably on-brand while adapting to different markets, languages and retail environments. For the MINI Clubman, the challenge was to preserve the distinctive global campaign idea and characteristic MINI tone across local executions.

My role

As Art Director, I worked on the international adaptation of the MINI Clubman launch campaign. My responsibility was to ensure that the campaign idea, visual language and tone remained consistent while accommodating the requirements of different markets and formats.

Approach

The global creative platform was translated across multiple campaign motifs, languages and retail touchpoints. The focus was on maintaining the strength of the original idea while ensuring that each execution worked within its respective market context.

Outcome

The result was a consistent international campaign system that allowed the MINI Clubman launch to be adapted across markets without losing the distinctive character of the brand.
