MINI Clubman

The Other
MINI.

International launch communication that kept the MINI idea, image language and tone consistent while adapting the campaign across markets and motifs.

Role
Art Director
Agency
Interone BBDO
Client
MINI

Task

International adaptation of the launch campaign for the MINI Clubman. The new model had to be introduced in different markets without losing the independent brand identity and characteristic MINI tone.

My contribution

  • Creative development of the international campaign adaptation
  • Transfer of the central brand idea to different markets and motifs
  • Design of print, retail and sales-promotion materials
  • Consistent MINI tone and image language across executions
  • Collaboration with international teams and market organisations

Result

  • Consistent launch communication across international markets
  • Adaptation of one campaign idea across different motifs and languages
  • Retail and product-launch communication with a clear Clubman identity
