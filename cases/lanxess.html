Work 05 / LANXESS

LANXESS

Making the
unseen visible.

An awareness campaign revealing where LANXESS materials play a role in mobility — connecting technical expertise with recognisable products and applications.

Role
Senior Art Director
Agency
Liquid Campaign
Client
LANXESS

Challenge

Technical materials and industrial expertise often remain invisible to the people who benefit from them. For LANXESS, the communication challenge was to make the company’s role in mobility tangible by showing where its materials appear in recognisable products and applications.

My role

As Senior Art Director at Liquid Campaign, I was responsible for campaign development and the visual direction of the communication. My task was to translate an abstract and technically complex subject into a visual story that could be understood quickly.

Approach

The campaign connected technical materials with familiar mobility applications, making the relationship between material expertise and everyday products more visible. The concept was developed across a family of mobility motifs and extended into large-format outdoor communication, including billboards in Davos.

Outcome

The result was a campaign that gave an abstract B2B topic a clear and accessible visual narrative and created a consistent communication platform across different applications.
